About JDB Woodworks

Superior service, personalized attention

JDB Woodworks is a veteran-owned and operated custom woodworking business specializing in handcrafted furniture, custom wood projects, and professional furniture assembly. Founded in 2022, we proudly serve homeowners, businesses, and contractors looking for high-quality, made-to-order woodworking with attention to detail and long-lasting craftsmanship.

We design and build custom furniture, including tables, shelves, desks, storage solutions, benches, and home décor, using quality hardwoods and durable construction methods. Every project is tailored to fit your space, style, and budget, whether you are looking for rustic, modern, farmhouse, or traditional woodworking designs.

In addition to custom builds, JDB Woodworks offers reliable furniture assembly services, helping customers safely and correctly assemble flat-pack furniture, office furniture, retail fixtures, and home furnishings. We work with popular brands and custom pieces alike, ensuring everything is assembled, leveled, and ready to use.

As a small, locally operated woodworking shop, we take pride in honest pricing, clear communication, and dependable service. Our goal is to deliver results that exceed expectations and stand the test of time.

Restoration & repair

Furniture restoration & antique furniture repair

At JDB Woodworks, we believe that quality craftsmanship and beautiful furniture deserve to stand the test of time. We specialize in furniture restoration and antique furniture repair, bringing new life to cherished pieces while preserving their original character, craftsmanship, and history.

Whether you have an antique family heirloom that needs attention, a piece of furniture that has seen better days, or a treasured item you’d like restored to its former beauty, we take pride in giving each project the care and attention it deserves.

From repairing and restoring older furniture to refreshing worn finishes and bringing neglected pieces back to life, our goal is to preserve the beauty of the past while creating something you can enjoy for years to come.

Don’t throw away a piece worth saving. Let JDB Woodworks help bring it back to life!
